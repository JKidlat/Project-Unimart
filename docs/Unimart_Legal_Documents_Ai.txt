@2 Business model reflected in this pack
Unimart is a Ghana-focused online marketplace connecting buyers with independent student sellers. Any person aged 18 or older may browse, register as a buyer and purchase. Buyers do not need to be tertiary students or complete student verification. Only currently enrolled students of recognised/accredited tertiary institutions in Ghana may sell. Sellers must submit a student ID and use an institutional email address. Administrators manually review the ID and verify the institutional email. Student sellers submit listings for administrator review. Listings are not visible to the public until approved. Sellers and buyers transact directly. At launch, Unimart does not collect, hold, escrow, split, or distribute purchase money. Sellers may accept cash, Mobile Money or bank transfer directly, and choose which methods they accept. The chosen method and payment instructions must be clear before the parties commit. Sellers arrange delivery or collection and disclose delivery costs, timing and arrangements. Unimart is initially free. No listing fee, commission or subscription is charged at launch. Google Analytics and Google Ads are intended tracking integrations. The website must verify which tags are actually installed and must not activate non-essential tracking before the appropriate consent is obtained. Paystack is a possible future integration. It is not part of the initial payment model described here. These documents must be reviewed and updated before enabling payment collection, checkout, split settlement, refunds, or any flow in which Unimart receives or controls funds. Unimart is an intermediary platform. Buyers and sellers contract directly with each other for the sale of a product. This description does not remove any responsibility that applicable law places on Unimart or any seller.
